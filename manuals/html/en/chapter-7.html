7. Shop, orders, payments, subscriptions and donations

Chapter 7: Shop, orders, payments, subscriptions and donations

easy2edit offers a set of plugins that work together to sell products, manage orders, process payments and run subscriptions or donation campaigns. Depending on what you want to offer on your site, you combine the Webshop, Orders, Financieel and Abonnementen plugins. Understanding how these plugins depend on each other will help you set everything up correctly.

How the plugins work together#

  • Webshop needs the Orders and Betalen (Payments) plugins to function.
  • Orders can work on its own, for example if you only want to let customers pay by invoice. The Betalen and Webshop plugins both need Orders to work.
  • Financieel brings together everything related to money: payment methods, VAT rates, shipping options and the layout of financial documents. Orders, Webshop and Abonnementen all need this plugin.
  • Abonnementen needs the Orders plugin, since every subscription period or donation generates an order.

In short, Orders is the central plugin for anything that results in a transaction, Financieel handles the money side, and Webshop and Abonnementen are the two ways customers can place an order.

Webshop#

The Webshop plugin lets you sell products online. You manage:

  • Products and product groups
  • Variants, such as colour or size, each with their own price
  • Staggered pricing (staffelprijzen), where the price changes depending on the quantity ordered
  • Shipping methods and discount codes

easy2edit includes demo content with ready-made shop pages: a products page, a shopping cart page and a checkout page. You can use these as a starting point and adjust them to fit your site.

To set up a basic webshop:

  1. Go to Admin > Plugins and activate Webshop, Orders and Betalen.
  2. Add your products and organise them into product groups.
  3. Create any variants your products need, such as size or colour, and set a price for each variant.
  4. Set up staggered pricing if you want to offer quantity discounts.
  5. Configure your shipping methods and, optionally, discount codes.
  6. Check the demo shop pages (products, shopping cart, checkout) and adjust them to match your site.

Orders#

The Orders plugin manages orders for the entire site, not just the webshop. It handles orders coming from the webshop, from subscriptions and from donations in one place. Here you manage:

  • Order status, so you can follow an order from placement to completion
  • VAT settings
  • The confirmation email sent to customers after an order is placed

Orders can also work independently of the Webshop plugin. This is useful if you only want to accept orders paid by invoice, without running a full online shop.

Financieel#

The Financieel plugin is where all money-related settings come together:

  • Payment methods and payment terms
  • The connections to the payment providers Stripe and Mollie
  • VAT rates, with a standard high and low rate that you link to your products
  • Shipping options
  • The layout of invoices, quotes, packing slips and return slips

Since Orders, Webshop and Abonnementen all depend on Financieel, this plugin forms the financial backbone of any paid feature on your site.

Abonnementen (Subscriptions and donations)#

The Abonnementen plugin lets you offer subscription plans and donation campaigns.

Subscriptions give visitors access to parts of your site, such as pages, content, chat, video, likes or services. A subscription can be:

  • A one-time purchase
  • A recurring subscription, with a new order generated for each period

Donation campaigns let visitors choose an amount to give using a slider, moving between a minimum and a maximum value you set.

Actions determine what is free and what requires payment, so you can combine free and paid content on the same site using subscription plans.

To set up a subscription or donation:

  1. Activate the Abonnementen plugin, which requires Orders to be active.
  2. Create a subscription plan and decide whether it is a one-time purchase or a recurring subscription.
  3. Link the plan to the pages, content, chat, video, likes or services it should unlock.
  4. For donations, set up a donation action and define the minimum and maximum amount for the slider.
  5. Use actions to decide which parts of your site remain free and which require a subscription or donation.

Payments and the sandbox#

Payments made through the Webshop, Orders or Abonnementen plugins are processed through Stripe or Mollie, both configured in the Financieel plugin. Before going live, you can use the sandbox to simulate payments. This lets you test the full order and payment flow, from checkout to confirmation, without processing any real transactions.
