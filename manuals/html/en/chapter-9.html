9. SEO, FAQ and statistics

SEO#

Easy2edit helps you make your website easy to find, not only for traditional search engines but also for bots and AI agents that read and summarise web content. The SEO tools in Easy2edit take care of the technical basics as well as the content details that search engines and AI systems look for.

Out of the box, Easy2edit takes care of:

  • robots.txt, which tells search engines and other bots which parts of your site they may or may not visit.
  • A sitemap, an overview of all your pages that helps search engines find and index your content efficiently.
  • llms.txt, a file aimed at AI agents and language models, describing your site in a way that helps them understand and reference it correctly.
  • Page titles and descriptions, the short pieces of text that appear in search results and that explain what a page is about.
  • Structured data, extra information added to your pages that helps search engines and AI agents understand the meaning of your content, such as what is a product, an article, a question, or an event.

As a website owner, you generally do not need to build these elements yourself. Easy2edit generates and maintains them automatically based on the content of your pages, modules and plugins. This means that as you add or update pages, your SEO information stays up to date without extra manual work.

FAQ#

The FAQ module lets you publish your own frequently asked questions and answers on your website, on the page /faq.

Key points about the FAQ module:

  • You create and manage your own questions and answers, tailored to your organisation and your visitors.
  • Questions and answers can be translated, so visitors can read your FAQ in their own language.
  • If your website was imported from an existing site that already had frequently asked questions, those questions are automatically placed into this FAQ module, so you do not have to recreate them by hand.
  • The FAQ content lives on the /faq page.

To make sure visitors can actually find your FAQ, add the page to your site menu:

  1. Go to Admin > Pages.
  2. Locate the FAQ page, or create it if it does not yet exist.
  3. Add the page to the relevant menu so it appears in your site navigation.

Because the FAQ page is a normal page within Pages, you manage its placement in the menu the same way as any other page on your site.

Statistics#

The Statistics area gives you a clear overview of what is happening on your website, combining graphs and figures from across your entire site in one place.

The statistics overview includes information such as:

  • Visitors, so you can see how many people are visiting your site and how that develops over time.
  • SEO, giving insight into how your site is performing in terms of search engine visibility.
  • Bots, showing activity from automated crawlers visiting your website.
  • AI calls, showing how often AI agents or language models access your content.

In addition to these overall figures, Statistics also brings together data from the rest of your website:

  • Every module and every plugin that collects data gets its own section, or tab, within Statistics.
  • Modules and plugins send their figures directly to this central statistics area, so you do not need to check many different places to understand how your site is doing.
  • The statistics are presented using widgets with charts, making trends and patterns easy to read at a glance.

One important thing to keep in mind is that statistics depend on the module or plugin being active:

  • If a particular plugin is switched off, it will not collect or send any data to Statistics.
  • This means that no figures will be counted or shown for that plugin while it is disabled.
  • If you want to track a certain type of activity, make sure the related module or plugin is enabled.

Together, SEO, FAQ and Statistics help you present your website clearly to visitors, search engines and AI agents, while also giving you the insight you need to understand how your site is actually being used.
