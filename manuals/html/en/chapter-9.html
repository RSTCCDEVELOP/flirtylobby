9. SEO, FAQ and statistics

SEO

Easy2edit helps your website get found and understood, not only by traditional search engines but also by bots and AI agents. The SEO tools take care of the technical and content-related elements that search engines and AI systems use to index and interpret your site.

The following elements are managed for you:

  • robots.txt, which tells search engines and bots which parts of your site they may or may not visit
  • A sitemap, listing your pages so search engines can find and index them efficiently
  • llms.txt, a file aimed at AI agents and language models, helping them understand your site
  • Page titles and descriptions, shown in search results and used by bots and AI agents to understand what a page is about
  • Structured data, which adds machine-readable information to your pages so search engines and AI agents can better interpret their content

Because these elements are generated and maintained by Easy2edit, you do not need to manually create or update robots.txt, sitemaps or structured data. The system keeps them in line with your site structure and content as it changes.

FAQ

The FAQ module lets you publish your own frequently asked questions and answers on your website, available at the page /faq.

Key points about the FAQ module:

  • You create and maintain the questions and answers yourself
  • Questions and answers can be translated into all languages your site supports
  • If a site was imported into Easy2edit, questions and answers that were found during the import are automatically placed into this module, so you do not have to recreate them manually
  • The FAQ lives on its own page, /faq

To make sure visitors can find your FAQ page, add it to your site's menu.

  1. Go to Admin > Pages
  2. Locate or confirm the /faq page
  3. Add this page to the menu so visitors can navigate to it directly

Once the FAQ page is in the menu, visitors can browse your questions and answers like any other page on your site.

Statistics

The statistics section gives you an overview of how your website is performing, combining charts and figures in one place. It covers the whole site, including:

  • Visitor numbers
  • SEO performance
  • Bot activity
  • AI calls, such as requests coming from AI agents visiting your site

Statistics are organized clearly so you can see the overall picture as well as detailed information per module and plugin.

Each module and plugin that supports statistics gets its own tab or section within the statistics area. Modules and plugins write their own figures into this section, so you always see numbers that are relevant to the specific functionality you use.

An important detail to understand is that statistics depend on the module or plugin being active. If a particular plugin is switched off, nothing will be counted or shown for it in the statistics section. Turning a plugin back on resumes the counting, but data is not collected while it is disabled.

The statistics area uses widgets with charts to present the numbers visually, making it easy to spot trends in visitors, SEO performance, bot traffic and AI activity at a glance.

Working with SEO, FAQ and statistics together

These three areas complement each other. Good SEO settings, including page titles, descriptions and structured data, make your pages easier to find and understand, both for traditional search engines and for AI agents. The FAQ module adds clear, structured content that can also be picked up by search engines and AI systems, strengthening your site's relevance for common questions. The statistics section then shows you the results, letting you see how visitors, bots and AI agents are actually interacting with your site.

A practical way to use these tools together:

  1. Review your SEO settings to make sure page titles and descriptions are clear and accurate for your key pages
  2. Build out your FAQ page with questions that reflect what your visitors commonly ask, and add it to your menu under Admin > Pages
  3. Check the statistics section regularly to see how visitors, SEO and bot or AI activity are evolving over time
  4. Use the per module and per plugin tabs in statistics to understand how specific features of your site are performing, keeping in mind that only active plugins will report figures

By keeping an eye on all three areas, you can continuously improve how your website is found, how well it answers visitor questions, and how it performs over time.
