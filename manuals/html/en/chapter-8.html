8. Community, marketplace, agenda, documents and games

This chapter covers the plugins that bring your members together: profiles, dating-style discovery, messaging, chat, direct connections, a marketplace, an agenda, document management, a games hall, showcase presentations, and the related age, country and gambling checks.

Video: Community and marketplace (English voice-over also in Nederlands)

Community#

The Community plugin brings together everything you need for a community or dating site: profile pages with photos, a member search, blocking unwanted users, searching and matching (liking, favorites, visitors), private messages, chat, and a direct connection via WebRTC using a code, link or QR code.

Community works with profile themes, profile fields and "available for" options, so you can tailor the profile experience to your audience. When parts of the community should require a subscription or a donation, Community works together with the Subscriptions plugin.

Profile themes#

Profile themes define how member profiles look and which fields they have. easy2edit comes with ready-made theme packages: Sporters, Dating, Business and Webshop. You can install or remove these packages, or build your own theme with its own fields, visibility settings and search filters.

The superadmin decides which themes administrators and users are allowed to choose. Themes can be exported and imported as JSON, which makes it easy to reuse or share a theme between sites.

Community building blocks#

  • Profiles (profiles): a personal profile page with multiple photos, visibility per language and group, a member search, and the ability to block other users.
  • Discover (discover): search and meet other users as on a dating site, with filters, recommendations, liking, matches, favorites and visitors.
  • Messages (messages): a messaging system between users, with its own inbox.
  • Chat (chat): chat in a popup or full screen, either through the portal or directly via WebRTC, including photos, video and video chat.
  • Direct connection (directconnect): a direct WebRTC connection between two people using a code, link or QR code, even without an account. It supports chat, photos, video and video chat, and is available on the pages /directconnect and /direct.

Marketplace#

The marketplace lets members offer products, services or anything else for sale, each with a theme and images. An item can have:

  • a fixed price
  • bidding, starting from a minimum price
  • a booking, for example an appointment or signing up for an event

Bids are visible to all members, so everyone can follow how an item is doing.

Agenda#

The agenda gives users and groups a shared calendar. It supports:

  • personal appointments
  • appointments with other members, including invitations, confirmations or proposing another time
  • group appointments
  • events created by administrators, which can be placed on pages as a system object

The agenda includes reminders, notifications and a tile on the dashboard, so members are kept up to date on what is coming up.

Document management#

Document management lets you upload files into a folder structure, with tags, versions and rights per user or group. Files can be stored on the server or on S3, and can be encrypted for extra protection.

Blocks are available to show documents directly on pages, so you can present shared files, manuals or member documents wherever needed.

Games#

The games hall offers a space for your members to play. Each game is its own package, delivered as a folder or zip containing a game.json file, placed in the games folder of this plugin. A game has its own configuration for management and its own settings per member.

Games run in a sandboxed environment and keep track of scores and progress. The games hall supports ranglijsten (leaderboards) and comes with an example game to show how it works.

Age, country and gambling checks#

For sites that need it, easy2edit offers an age check (18+), a country check, and a gambling popup (24+). These are only meant for target groups where such checks are required or desired.

The settings for these checks are found under Beveiliging (Security). The popups and the country check only work when this plugin is switched on.

Showcase#

Showcase lets you present your projects to visitors. Each project can include a title, a short text, a description, a goal, a link, a duration and a budget, together with pictures and videos (YouTube, Vimeo or an mp4/webm link). Every one of these fields can be switched on or off in the plugin settings, shown as columns of switches.

Projects also have a status (active, in development, offline), shared tags for searching, and groups that can have subgroups; choosing a group automatically includes its subgroups.

The admin page is organized in four tabs:

  • Projects
  • Groups
  • Tags
  • Presentations

A project itself is edited in a popup with the tabs General, Content, Media and Translations.

A presentation decides how a selection of projects is displayed: as a grid, list, table, photo wall (masonry), scroller or slider. A project can open on its own page (/showcase/<name>), in a popup, or in place on the same page. Presentations can include a search field and filters on tags, groups and status, and can be limited to certain fields, a specific group, specific tags, a specific status, or only featured projects.

Every active presentation is a system object, so you can place it on a page in edit mode. Three standard presentations always exist: search, scroller and list.

Public pages are available at /showcase and /showcase/<name>.

To help you explore Showcase quickly, demo content is available: 9 projects, groups, tags and 6 presentations. You can add or remove this demo content with a single button on the plugin page.
