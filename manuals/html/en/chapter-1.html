1. Introduction

Easy2edit is a simple, modular management system (CMS) for building and running portals, websites, webshops and communities. It is designed so that you can start quickly with a ready-made setup, and then grow the site by switching modules and plugins on or off, without needing to touch any code.

Behind the scenes, Easy2edit runs on a Django (REST) backend and a Next.js frontend. You do not need to know anything about these technologies to use the system. What matters for you as an administrator or website owner is that the site is built from blocks, and that features of the site can be turned on or off as modules and plugins.

How a site is built

Rather than writing pages from scratch, you build a website from blocks. These blocks are combined into pages, and pages are combined into a complete site. On top of that, you decide which parts of the system are active by switching modules and plugins on or off. This makes it possible to keep a site simple and light, or to expand it with more functionality as your needs grow.

Who uses Easy2edit

There are three kinds of people who work with an Easy2edit site:

  • Users: visitors who have created an account on the site. They can log in, manage their own profile and use the features that are available to them.
  • Admins: people who manage parts of the site. What an admin is allowed to do depends on the roles assigned to them. One admin might only manage pages, while another might also manage plugins or users, depending on their role.
  • Superadmins: the owner or reseller of the site. A superadmin can do everything, including changing system settings, switching modules and plugins on or off, managing prices and managing the licence.

This structure means that you can safely delegate day to day management tasks to admins, while keeping full control over the system as a superadmin.

The dashboard

After logging in, you work from the dashboard. The dashboard is organised into three areas:

  • Dashboard: this is your own personal area, where you manage your own profile, see your notifications and view your own AI credit.
  • Admin: this is the management area, where admins manage the parts of the site they are responsible for.
  • Super admin: this is the system area, reserved for superadmins, where system-wide settings are configured.

Which of these areas you see, and what you can do inside them, depends on whether you are a user, an admin or a superadmin.

Modules and plugins

Easy2edit is modular, which means that functionality is organised into modules and plugins that can be switched on or off as needed.

Modules are parts of the system that can be enabled or disabled. You manage them under Super admin > Modules.

Plugins are signed extension packages. They bring their own pages and their own modules with them, extending what the site can do. You manage plugins under Admin > Plugins.

Because modules and plugins can be switched on or off, you only need to activate the parts of the system that your site actually needs, keeping things clear and manageable.

Standard installations

To help you get started quickly, Easy2edit offers standard installations. A standard installation sets up the modules, plugins and theme for a particular type of website with a single click. Available types include:

  • Standard website
  • Web shop
  • Company website
  • Business network
  • Intranet
  • Dating platform
  • Sports club
  • Foundation
  • Marketplace
  • Game hall

Each standard installation is shown as a small tile, visible in the admin area. The tile has a settings pop-up, which is offered the first time you install it. From this pop-up you can configure things such as AI credit settings, which modules are included, and whether demo data is added.

The header

At the top of the screen, the header gives you quick access to a few general controls:

  • The language switcher, to change the display language.
  • The edit-mode switch (shown as a pencil icon), to turn editing of the site on or off.
  • Light and dark mode, to switch the appearance of the interface.
  • The user menu, where you can access your account options.

These header controls are available wherever you are in the system, so you can always adjust your language, switch between viewing and editing the site, change the display mode, or access your user menu.
